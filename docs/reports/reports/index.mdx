Device42 provides several tools for reporting on and analyzing your infrastructure data, from interactive dashboards and visual analytics to scheduled reports and audit logs.

Device42 provides Standard Reports and Insights+ for new reports. Advanced Reporting is being phased out:

- **[Standard Reports](reports/reports/standard-reports.mdx):** Simple tabular reports for quick queries (formerly known as "Classic Reports").
- **[Insights+](reports/reports/insights-plus.mdx):** The recommended option for new dashboards and analytics. A BI platform for interactive dashboards and visuals with deep data exploration capabilities.
- **[Advanced Reporting](reports/reports/advanced-reporting.mdx):** This feature will be removed from the product in a future release; do not use it to create new reports.

Administrators can [hide the Advanced Reporting menu](reports/reports/advanced-reporting.mdx#hide-the-advanced-reporting-menu) for all users.

## Choose a Reporting Tool

For new reports, choose a tool based on how you want to use the data:

| Tool | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **[Standard Reports](reports/reports/standard-reports.mdx)** | Creating reusable tabular reports that you can filter, export, print, or send on a schedule. |
| **[Insights+](reports/reports/insights-plus.mdx)** | Exploring trends and relationships with interactive dashboards, charts, and other visualizations. |

### Advanced Reporting

**[Advanced Reporting](reports/reports/advanced-reporting.mdx)** is being phased out and will be removed in a future release. Do not use it to create new reports; use Standard Reports or Insights+ instead.

This section also covers [alerts and notifications](reports/reports/setup-alerts-and-notifications.mdx), [discovery scores](reports/reports/discovery-quality-scores.mdx), [audit logs](reports/reports/object-history-aka-audit-log.mdx), and tools for exporting data.
